## 3. Stakeholder Reflection: Quick Chat & Q Topic vs. Published Dashboard

1. **Where did Q agree with the Published Dashboard?**  
   For every single-domain question (`T1` channel conversion `48.99%`, `T4` priority resolution `1.79` vs `1.97` days, `T5` campaign spend `$12.36M` vs `$1.13M`), the configured `NovaTech Revenue Intelligence` Q Topic matched the published dashboard **100%**.
2. **Where did Q disagree or struggle, and why?**  
   Two real-world limitations surfaced during live testing:
   - **Metric Definition Ambiguity (`T2`):** Q Topic averaged `$0` Lost deals into `deal_value` (`$1,417.24`), whereas business leadership measures average won deal size (`$2,245.08`).
   - **Cross-Dataset Join Boundary (`T3`):** Classic Q Topics query one dataset per question (`crm_deals` OR `support_tickets`), so combining ticket volume with CRM revenue triggered a missing-field warning. Meanwhile, raw many-to-many joins (`63,420` rows) inflate naive `SUM(deal_value)` by `142.68x` unless governed by **Level-Aware Calculations (`LAC`)**.
3. **Recommended Operating Model for VP Sarah Chen:**  
   - Use the **Published 3-Sheet Dashboard** (`NovaTech Revenue Intelligence Dashboard`) for recurring Monday revenue reviews, cross-domain Account Health (`LAC` deduplicated revenue), and interactive regional/channel slicing.
   - Use **Quick Chat & the Q Topic** for fast single-domain ad-hoc follow-ups and automated Executive Summaries (`Create -> Executive summary`), always verifying metric definitions (Won vs. All Deals) before executive action.
