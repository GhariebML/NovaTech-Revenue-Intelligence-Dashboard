## Reflection (Included in Executive Report Summary)

1. **Where did Quick Chat (Q) agree with the dashboard?**  
   After configuring the `NovaTech Revenue Intelligence` Topic (adding business synonyms, friendly names, default aggregations, and `Won Deals Only` / `At-Risk Account` named filters), Q matched the dashboard 100% across all single-domain aggregations: regional win rates, channel conversion rates, campaign ROI, ticket priority resolution averages, and product area sentiment distributions.

2. **Where did Q disagree or struggle, and why?**  
   Before Topic configuration, Q struggled in three predictable ways: (a) it averaged `$0` Lost deals into `deal_value` (`$1,417.24` instead of `$2,245.08` Won average), (b) it confused `campaign_response` (`27.19%`) with `Closed Won` funnel conversion (`18.39%`), and (c) most critically, querying `SUM(deal_value)` over the `63,420`-row `novatech_unified` join multiplied CRM revenue by `142.68x` (`$100,900,682` vs. true `$707,201`) because accounts with multiple deals, marketing leads, and support tickets fan out across rows. Additionally, Q's naive count of `customer_sentiment` initially obscured the `59` undocumented null entries on open tickets until explicitly checked.

3. **When should NovaTech use Quick Chat vs. the Dashboard?**  
   - **Use the Published Dashboard** for recurring Monday morning revenue reviews, standardized KPI tracking, executive visual storytelling, and safe cross-filtering where pre-built level-aware calculations guarantee financial accuracy.
   - **Use Quick Chat (Q)** for ad-hoc, cross-domain exploratory follow-ups that aren't pre-built as dashboard widgets (e.g., *"Which Telecom Enterprise accounts have >15 tickets in the last 30 days and lost deals due to Competitor Won?"*)—always cross-checking cross-table financial sums against source CRM totals before making budget commitments.
