## Executive Snapshot Across All Three Systems

NovaTech's unified revenue intelligence layer connects **`2,240` marketing leads**, **`499` CRM sales deals (`85` accounts)**, and **`3,000` customer support tickets** inside Amazon Quick's in-memory engine (`SPICE`). Cross-domain reporting eliminates manual Monday morning spreadsheet merges and surfaces four headline metrics for leadership:

- **Marketing Funnel (`novatech_marketing_campaigns`):** NovaTech invested **`$12,359,497.34`** across 6 campaigns and 5 channels, generating **`$1,127,223.09`** in direct attributed revenue (**`-90.88%` aggregate return on investment**). Overall lead response reached **`27.19%`** (`609` responses) and Closed-Won conversion reached **`18.39%`** (`412` converted leads). **Direct Mail** outperformed all other channels at **`48.99%` conversion** (`-70.74%` ROI), whereas **Organic Search** converted only **`0.89%`** (`-97.79%` ROI).
- **Sales Pipeline (`novatech_crm_deals`):** NovaTech closed **`315` Won deals** (`63.13%` win rate) totaling **`$707,201.00`** in revenue (`$2,245.08` average won deal; `71.92` average days to close for won deals vs. `57.93` days for lost deals). **NovaPulse** drove **`77.57%` (`$548,560`)** of total revenue compared to **NovaEdge (`$158,641`)**. **Central** led regional win rate at **`69.90%`**, whereas manager performance spanned from **`70.8%`** (David Kim) down to **`50.0%`** (Brian Nakamura). Nearly half of all `184` losses (`46.7%`) stemmed from **No Decision Made (`43`)** and **Poor Product Fit (`43`)**.
- **Customer Health (`novatech_support_tickets` + Unified Account View):** Average resolution across `2,941` resolved tickets sits at **`1.94` days (`58.71` hours)**, with virtually zero prioritization between **Critical (`1.79` days)** and **Low (`1.97` days)** tickets. Linking CRM revenue to support friction identifies **9 High-Risk Enterprise accounts** holding **`$145,735.00` in Won revenue (`20.61%` of total company revenue)**, led by **YieldMax Software (`$40,722`, `334` tickets)**, **TrueNorth Electronics (`$16,747`, `180` tickets, `25.6%` negative sentiment)**, and **LionGate Holdings (`$16,275`, `176` tickets)**. Additionally, data-quality auditing uncovered **`59` undocumented blank `customer_sentiment` records (`1.97%`)** coinciding with unresolved open tickets.
