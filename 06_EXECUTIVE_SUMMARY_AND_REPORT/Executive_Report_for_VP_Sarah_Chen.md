## 1. Data Strategy, Quality Audit, and Unified Architecture

To eliminate NovaTech's Monday morning manual reporting workflow, we consolidated three disconnected business datasets inside Amazon Quick's Super-fast, Parallel, In-memory Calculation Engine (SPICE), which stores cleaned tables in memory for interactive querying. Our data verification audit across `499` Customer Relationship Management (CRM) deals (`85` customer accounts), `2,240` marketing campaign leads, and `3,000` customer support tickets confirmed overall structural alignment with the Data Dictionary while uncovering three critical quality conditions that directly shaped our architecture.

First, `15` unlinked account identifiers appear exclusively in marketing (`150` orphan lead rows, or `6.70%`) and support (`204` orphan ticket rows, or `6.80%`) without matching CRM accounts. Rather than discarding those leads and tickets permanently, we retained all three standalone source tables in SPICE and designated the CRM deals table as the **anchor table** (the primary reference table) for our unified cross-system dataset using left joins on the shared account identifier. Second, we identified an undocumented defect in the support dataset: while the Data Dictionary stated zero missing values for customer sentiment, exactly `59` tickets (`1.97%`) lack a sentiment score because those exact `59` tickets remain open without a resolution timestamp. Third, joining multiple deals, leads, and tickets per account expands `499` CRM deals into `63,420` combined rows—a **many-to-many join fan-out** (row multiplication across linked records) that inflates naive revenue totals by `142.68` times (turning `$707,201` of actual won revenue into `$100.90` million). Accordingly, we corrected date, currency, and country fields, added five plain-English calculated metrics (sales cycle length, deal win indicator, campaign return on investment, ticket resolution duration, and an account risk flag), and enforced strict **level-aware aggregation** (calculating financial totals at the unique opportunity or account level rather than across multiplied join rows).

## 2. Dashboard Design Rationale and Cross-View Interactivity

The Revenue Intelligence Dashboard translates VP Sarah Chen's requirements into three core interactive views—plus a fourth predictive forecast and join-audit view—built for rapid executive decisions:

- **Sheet 1 — Marketing Funnel:** Combines four executive Key Performance Indicator (KPI) cards with a funnel progression chart, a side-by-side channel response versus closed-won conversion comparison, a campaign spend-to-revenue return chart, and a customer segment matrix. Interactive filter controls for campaign name, channel, date range, and segment allow marketing leaders to isolate low-performing outreach immediately.
- **Sheet 2 — Sales Pipeline:** Displays total won revenue (`$707,201`), overall win rate (`63.13%`), average won deal size (`$2,245.08`), and average sales cycle duration (`66.76` days) alongside regional performance, account-tier product mix, primary loss reasons, and quarterly velocity trends. One-click chart filtering lets regional managers select a specific territory or loss driver and watch companion visuals update instantaneously.
- **Sheet 3 — Customer Health (Unified):** Evaluates support resolution speed by priority tier, ticket load and negative sentiment concentration across product areas, and an account-level **At-Risk Accounts** intelligence table fed by the unified dataset. Cross-sheet navigation actions connect each sheet directly so leadership can trace an Enterprise segment or specific high-value customer seamlessly from marketing touchpoint to post-sale ticket friction.

## 3. Impact of Semantic Topic Configuration on Quick Chat Accuracy

To support ad-hoc natural-language questions through Amazon Quick Chat (Q), we configured a custom semantic layer (a business-friendly translation dictionary known as a **Topic**) named *NovaTech Revenue Intelligence*. Before Topic configuration, Q struggled with three recurring errors: it averaged zero-dollar lost deals into overall deal value (understating average won deal size at `$1,417.24` instead of `$2,245.08`), treated initial marketing response rates (`27.19%`) as final closed-won conversions (`18.39%`), and summed multiplied revenue across the `63,420`-row joined dataset (reporting YieldMax Software at `$13.60` million instead of `$40,722`).

Configuring friendly field names, business synonyms (mapping phrases such as *win rate*, *sales cycle*, *campaign return*, and *churn risk*), default average aggregations for rates, explicit named filters for **Won Deals Only** and **Resolved Tickets Only**, and hiding noisy system identifiers transformed Q's reliability. Re-testing the same baseline questions post-Topic yielded `100%` numerical alignment with the Marketing Funnel, Sales Pipeline, and Customer Health dashboard visuals.

## 4. Key Business Insights and Recommended Actions

1. **Reallocate Marketing Capital Away from Negative-Return Digital Channels:** Across all six campaigns, NovaTech spent `$12.36` million to generate `$1.13` million in attributed revenue (`-90.88%` aggregate return on investment). Organic Search converted just `0.89%` of leads (`-97.79%` return), while **Direct Mail** converted `48.99%` (`53.02%` response rate) and **Partner Referrals** converted `31.10%`. *Action:* Pause incremental spend on Organic Search and `NovaEdge Awareness`, shift `30%` of quarterly budget into targeted Direct Mail and Partner co-selling for Enterprise buyers, and audit Partner Referral spend (`$7.27` million) alongside multi-year lifetime value attribution.
2. **Standardize Sales Coaching Across Underperforming Territories:** Central region achieved a `69.90%` win rate (`$274,285`), outperforming West (`58.79%`) and East (`57.81%`), driven by stark manager variance between David Kim (`70.8%` win rate) and Brian Nakamura (`50.0%`). *Action:* Replicate Central's qualification playbook across Brian Nakamura and Michael Chen (`51.4%`) to lift lagging regional conversion while preserving West's stronger `$2,559.21` average won deal size.
3. **Eliminate Late-Stage Pipeline Losses via Early Product-Fit Gating:** Nearly half (`46.7%`) of the `184` lost opportunities failed due to `No Decision Made` (`43` deals) or `Poor Product Fit` (`43` deals) after burning an average of `57.93` selling days. *Action:* Introduce a mandatory early-stage technical fit gate clarifying when prospects belong on the core `NovaPulse` suite (`77.6%` of revenue) versus `NovaEdge` lightweight tools (`22.4%`).
4. **Overhaul Support Priority Tiering and Target Product Stability Defects:** Support resolution averages `1.95` days (`58.70` hours) across `2,941` closed tickets, yet Critical tickets take `1.79` days compared to `1.97` days for Low-priority tickets—a difference of barely four hours. Furthermore, **Authentication** (`144` negative tickets) and **Data Pipeline** (`27.4%` negative rate) generate the steepest customer dissatisfaction. *Action:* Enforce an automated four-hour Service Level Agreement (SLA) escalation target for Critical outages and dedicate immediate engineering reliability sprints to Authentication and Data Pipeline stability.
5. **Protect $145,735 in High-Risk Enterprise Revenue:** Cross-system linking exposes nine high-risk Enterprise accounts combining `80` or more support tickets with elevated negative sentiment (`18.9%` to `31.1%`), collectively representing `20.61%` of NovaTech's won revenue—led by **YieldMax Software** (`$40,722`, `334` tickets), **TrueNorth Electronics** (`$16,747`, `180` tickets, `25.6%` negative), and **LionGate Holdings** (`$16,275`, `176` tickets). *Action:* Assign dedicated Customer Success recovery plans and executive sponsor check-ins to these top nine accounts prior to renewal cycles.

## 5. AI Analysis vs. Dashboard: Agreement, Discrepancies, and Governance

Across seven verified cross-domain questions, the post-Topic Quick Chat assistant agreed completely with our published dashboard KPIs on single-system metrics (channel conversions, regional win rates, priority resolution times, and product-area friction). Disagreements occurred strictly when unguarded natural-language queries triggered join fan-out across `63,420` rows, included `$0` lost deals in averages, or masked the `59` blank sentiment entries on unresolved tickets.

Going forward, NovaTech's revenue operations team should use the **published dashboard** as the authoritative system of record for weekly Monday KPI reviews, financial totals, and structured cross-filtering, while leveraging **Quick Chat (Q)** for rapid ad-hoc exploration and root-cause follow-ups—subject to verifying any cross-system revenue figure against CRM source totals before executive decision-making.
